Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This page is a general summary. Orbio Ltd should have it reviewed by a qualified solicitor before launch.

Overview

When Orbio Ltd handles calls, bookings and customer information on behalf of a client, the client is the data controller and Orbio Ltd acts as a data processor. We sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with every client before go-live.

What the DPA covers

  • The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing.
  • The types of personal data and categories of people involved, such as passengers, drivers and corporate contacts.
  • Processing only on the client’s documented instructions.
  • Confidentiality commitments for all agents.
  • Appropriate security measures, including working on the client’s own systems through a secure VPN or RDP connection.
  • Rules on using sub-processors and on international transfers.
  • Help with data subject requests and with breach notification.
  • Return or deletion of data when the service ends.
