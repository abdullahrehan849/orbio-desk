What we collect

When you send us a proposal request, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, company, phone number, email address, city, fleet size, the software you use and your message.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a tailored quote (steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interests in running our business).
  • To keep records of our communications and to protect our business from fraud and misuse (legitimate interests).

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for automated decision making.

Who we share it with

Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms, our form service provider, which passes them to our email inbox. Web3Forms may process data outside the UK. We rely on appropriate safeguards where required by UK data protection law. We may also share data with professional advisers or authorities where the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your request and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, then delete them. If you become a client, your data is kept in line with our contract with you.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your data, to object to its use, and to data portability, where these apply. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we use your data, please contact us first. You may also complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk or by phone on 0303 123 1113.
