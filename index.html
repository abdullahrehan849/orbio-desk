Privacy Policy and UK GDPR

What we collect

Enquiry details you send us, and, as a processor for our operator clients, booking and call data from their customers.

How long we keep it

Call recordings are kept for 90 days, then deleted. Enquiries are deleted when no longer needed. This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time: info@orbioltd.co.uk. We do not sell data.

Data Processing Agreement

We sign a Data Processing Agreement with every operator before go-live.

The operator is the controller and Orbio Ltd is the processor. The DPA covers processing only on your instructions, confidentiality (every agent signs an NDA), security measures, call recordings kept for 90 days then deleted, help with data subject requests, prompt notice of any breach, and deletion or return of data when the service ends.
