Our approach

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites. It aims to store nothing on your device other than what is needed for it to work.

Third-party services

  • WhatsApp. If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, you will be taken to WhatsApp, which has its own privacy and cookie policies.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not stop this website from working. If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.
