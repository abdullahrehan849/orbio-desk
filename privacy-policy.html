Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

ORBIO ASSIST is a brand of ORBIO LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (Company Number 17473713), with its registered office at 2 Frederick Street, Kings Cross, London, WC1X 0ND, United Kingdom. ORBIO LTD is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), registration reference ZC298303.

This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you use this website or contact us. It applies to visitors and prospective clients.

Who is responsible for your information

ORBIO LTD is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

Email info@orbioltd.co.uk or support@orbioltd.co.uk, call 020 8058 0504, or write to us at the registered office above.

What we collect

When you send us a proposal request we collect the information you give us: your name, company name, email address, telephone number (if you provide it) and the details of what you need help with. If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp we also keep the details of that conversation.

Our website does not ask for payment details, and we do not knowingly collect special category personal data through the enquiry form. Please do not include sensitive information in your message.

How we use it and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal. Lawful basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries.
  • To provide services under an agreed proposal. Lawful basis: performing a contract.
  • To keep our records, meet legal obligations and protect our legal rights. Lawful basis: legal obligation and legitimate interests.
  • To contact you about your enquiry. We do not send marketing emails without a lawful basis, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where needed with service providers that help us run our business, for example the form service that delivers your enquiry to us (Web3Forms), email and telephone providers, and professional advisers. We may also share information where required by law or by a regulator.

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, we take steps to make sure appropriate safeguards are in place.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including any legal, accounting or reporting requirements. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for access to your information, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to our use of it, and to data portability where it applies. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, and we limit access to people who need it. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please take care with what you send.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
