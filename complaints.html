We want to get things right. If you are not happy with our service or how we have handled your information, please tell us so we can put it right.

How to complain

Contact us in any of these ways and tell us what happened, what you would like us to do, and how to reach you.

Email info@orbioltd.co.uk or support@orbioltd.co.uk, call 020 8058 0504, or write to us at the registered office above.

What happens next

  • We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days.
  • We will look into it fairly and may ask you for more details.
  • We aim to send you a full written response within 28 days. If we need longer, we will explain why and keep you updated.

If you are still not satisfied

If you are not happy with our response, you can ask for it to be reviewed by a director of ORBIO LTD. We will reply to that review in writing.

Data protection complaints

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
